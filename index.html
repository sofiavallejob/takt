Rail networks as strings

Every route is a string, tuned by its length. The trains on the map are where they are right now, in Finland, Sweden or Norway. When one crosses another line or leaves it at a junction, that string sounds; when one reaches a station, its own line sounds softly. Longer routes play lower. The harmony moves on the hour, local time. Trains running late drag behind the beat and drift out of tune. Cancelled trains leave only a click.
